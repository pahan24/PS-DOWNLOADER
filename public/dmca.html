DMCA Policy

Last Updated: January 01, 2026  ·  Effective: January 01, 2026

⚖️ PS Downloader respects intellectual property rights. We comply with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and respond promptly to valid copyright infringement notices. If you believe your copyrighted work has been infringed, please use the form below.

Our DMCA Policy

PS Downloader is a tool that facilitates access to publicly available media on third-party platforms. We do not host, store, or distribute any media content on our servers. All content remains on and is served from the originating platforms.

However, we take copyright concerns seriously. If you are a copyright holder and believe PS Downloader is being used in a way that infringes your rights, you may submit a DMCA takedown notice. We will review valid notices within 72 business hours.

What Qualifies for a DMCA Notice?

  • You are the copyright owner or authorized agent of the copyright owner
  • A specific copyrighted work has been infringed
  • You can identify the specific infringing material or activity
  • You have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized

⚠️ False DMCA claims are a violation of the DMCA itself and may expose you to liability. Only submit a notice if you are the rightful copyright holder or authorized agent.

Our Response Process

  • Receive & Review: We receive your DMCA notice and review it for completeness and validity within 72 hours.
  • Verify Claim: We verify that the claimed content actually exists and assess the legitimacy of the claim.
  • Take Action: If valid, we take appropriate action which may include blocking access to specific URLs through our service.
  • Notify Uploader: Where applicable, we notify the user who submitted the URL about the DMCA notice.
  • Counter-Notice: The affected user may submit a counter-notice if they believe the claim is invalid.
  • Resolve: If no counter-notice is received within 14 business days, the restriction remains in place permanently.

Required Information for DMCA Notice

A valid DMCA notice must include all of the following under penalty of perjury:

  • Your full legal name and contact information (email, address, phone)
  • A description of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed
  • Identification of the specific infringing URL or material on PS Downloader
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is unauthorized
  • A statement that the information in the notice is accurate
  • Your physical or electronic signature

Counter-Notice Policy

If your URL was restricted due to a DMCA notice and you believe the claim was made in error, you may submit a counter-notice. Your counter-notice must include:

  • Your full name, address, phone number, and email
  • Identification of the specific restricted material and its former URL
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed by mistake
  • A statement consenting to jurisdiction
  • Your physical or electronic signature

Send counter-notices to: dmca@psdownloader.com

Repeat Infringer Policy

In accordance with the DMCA and our commitment to copyright protection, PS Downloader maintains a policy of terminating access for users who are determined to be repeat copyright infringers, in appropriate circumstances.
