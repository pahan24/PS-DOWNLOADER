Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 01, 2026  ·  Effective: January 01, 2026

🔒 Short version: PS Downloader does not collect, store, or sell your personal data. We do not require registration. The URLs you submit are processed in real-time and are not retained after your session ends.

1. Information We Collect

PS Downloader is designed to be privacy-first. We collect minimal information necessary to operate the service:

  • URLs you submit — processed in real-time to extract media information. Not stored after processing.
  • IP address — used temporarily for rate limiting (preventing abuse). Not linked to any personal profile.
  • Basic server logs — standard access logs (timestamp, HTTP method, response code) retained for up to 7 days for security monitoring, then automatically deleted.
  • Browser local storage — your recent URL history is stored locally in your own browser only. We have no access to this data.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • We do not require account registration or login
  • We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies
  • We do not build user profiles or track behavior over time
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to any third party
  • We do not use analytics services like Google Analytics
  • We do not store the media files you download — they go directly to your device

3. How We Use Information

The limited data we temporarily process is used solely to:

  • Process your media download requests in real-time
  • Apply rate limiting to prevent server abuse
  • Diagnose technical issues and maintain server security
  • Monitor for misuse of the service

4. Third-Party Services

PS Downloader uses yt-dlp (an open-source tool) to extract media information from URLs. When you submit a URL, our server requests information from the target platform (e.g., YouTube, TikTok) on your behalf. This is subject to those platforms' own privacy policies.

We use the following infrastructure:

  • Heroku — cloud hosting platform. Subject to Heroku's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — for typography. May log font requests. See Google's privacy policy.

5. Cookies

PS Downloader does not use tracking cookies. Your browser's localStorage is used to save your recent URL history locally on your device for convenience. This data never leaves your browser and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

6. Data Security

All connections to PS Downloader are secured with HTTPS/TLS encryption. We implement security headers (HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options) to protect against common web attacks. Since we do not store personal data, the risk of a data breach affecting you personally is minimized.

7. Children's Privacy

PS Downloader is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information through our service, please contact us immediately.

8. International Users

PS Downloader is operated globally. By using the service, you acknowledge that your URL submissions will be processed on servers that may be located outside your country of residence. We handle all data in accordance with this privacy policy regardless of location.
